kNN weather

Weather at any point on Earth, from the 5 nearest stations.
No server. Your location never leaves your browser.
~8,100 METAR stations, live observations.

choose a location

Distance-weighted average (kNN / IDW) of the 5 nearest METAR stations, corrected for elevation, pressure and wind direction (Nalder & Wein, 1998); method from SenseAI's weather tools (2015), where I was CTO.
Stations: NCAR/RAP stations.txt (Greg Thompson, NCAR/RAP). Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, api.weather.gov; elevation from Open-Meteo.
Source: github.com/idle-intelligence/trucs.ai/tree/main/knn-weather.
