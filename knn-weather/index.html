kNN weather

Inverse-distance-weighted kNN estimate of live weather at any point on Earth,
computed from the k nearest METAR stations. Runs client-side: haversine distance over ~8,100 stations, live observations fetched directly from the source APIs.

      Station list: NCAR/RAP stations.txt (Greg Thompson, NCAR/RAP), ~8,100 METAR-reporting stations.

      Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet (global ASOS/METAR currents), api.weather.gov (US stations), Open-Meteo (gridded forecast model, sampled at the point — not a station observation).
