kNN weather

Weather at any point on Earth, estimated from the 5 nearest live weather stations.
Runs entirely in your browser: distance search over ~8,100 stations, observations fetched live from the source APIs.
No model to download, no server.

choose a location

Distance-weighted average (kNN / IDW) of the 5 nearest METAR stations, corrected for elevation, pressure and wind direction (Nalder & Wein, 1998); method from SenseAI's weather tools (2015), where I was CTO.
Stations: NCAR/RAP stations.txt (Greg Thompson, NCAR/RAP). Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, api.weather.gov; comparison from Open-Meteo.
Source: github.com/idle-intelligence/trucs.ai/tree/main/knn-weather.
