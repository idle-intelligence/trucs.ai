Distance-weighted average (kNN / IDW) of up to 5 nearest METAR stations within 100 km, corrected for elevation, pressure and wind direction (Nalder & Wein, 1998); method from SenseAI's weather tools (2015), where I was CTO.
Stations: NCAR/RAP stations.txt (Greg Thompson, NCAR/RAP). Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, api.weather.gov; elevation from Open-Meteo. Map: country borders and cities from Natural Earth (public domain).
Source: github.com/idle-intelligence/trucs.ai/tree/main/knn-weather.
