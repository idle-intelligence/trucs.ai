kNN weather

Weather at any point on Earth, estimated from the nearest live METAR stations. Runs client-side: distance search over ~8,100 stations, live observations fetched directly from the source APIs.

calculation

For each variable, the estimate is the distance-weighted average of the neighbours that reported a value; missing values are left out.

method

Each value starts as the distance-weighted average of the neighbouring stations that reported it (closer stations weigh more). Temperature is then reduced to the target point's elevation with a lapse rate fitted by least squares across the neighbours (falling back to the standard atmosphere, −6.5 K/km, if there are fewer than three stations or the fit is implausible). Dew point is averaged as vapour pressure and converted back, since dew point is not linearly averageable. Pressure is averaged as sea-level pressure (QNH), then reduced to station pressure at the target elevation. Wind is averaged as vector components, not scalar speed and direction. Observations older than 60 minutes are excluded from these corrected averages.

Station list: NCAR/RAP stations.txt (Greg Thompson, NCAR/RAP), ~8,100 METAR-reporting stations.
Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet (global ASOS/METAR currents), api.weather.gov (US stations), Open-Meteo (numerical weather model, for comparison only).
Source: github.com/idle-intelligence/trucs.ai/tree/main/knn-weather.

kNN / IDW method from SenseAI's weather tools (2015), where I was CTO; elevation, vapour-pressure, QNH and vector-wind corrections after Nalder & Wein (1998) and standard practice.
